Requirement → Domain Thinking

### Goal

Convert a Product requirement into a **business/domain model** before thinking about code or database tables.

### Mental Flow

**Requirement → Why → Actors → Entities → Relationships → Flow → States → History → Rules → Model**

### Key Principle

Don't ask only:

> "What fields do I need?"

Ask:

> **"What real-world things exist, why do they exist, and how do they interact?"**

### Entity Test

A concept is likely an entity when it has:

* Identity
* Own attributes
* Relationships
* Lifecycle
* Independent business meaning/rules

**Persistence alone ≠ Entity.**
