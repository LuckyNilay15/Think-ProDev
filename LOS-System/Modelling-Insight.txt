# Page 3 — Important Modelling Insights

### 1. Business ≠ Application

Business exists independently.

One business can have multiple applications.

### 2. Person ≠ Business

A person acts on behalf of the business.

Their role may differ per application.

```text
Person → Application Relationship → Application
```

Possible roles:
**Owner / Applicant / Representative / Guarantor / Co-borrower**

### 3. Document ≠ Verification

Document can be:

**Uploaded → Rejected → Re-uploaded → Verified**

Therefore verification history may matter.

### 4. Product ≠ Offer

**Loan Product** = general configuration/rules.

**Loan Offer** = specific terms given to one application.

Product changes should not rewrite historical offers.

### 5. Current Status ≠ Status History

```text
Application
├── currentStatus
└── statusHistory[]
```

History can record:

**Previous Status → New Status → Who → When → Why**

### 6. Fact ≠ Just a Value

Example:

**Turnover = ₹1Cr**

may also require:

**Source + Evidence + Verification + Timestamp**

### Golden Question

> **"What business concept am I actually representing?"**
